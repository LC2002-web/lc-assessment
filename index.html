Background

樂歌 (LC) is a cross-church youth musical ministry established in 2002, built around four strategic aims — evangelism, standardization, talent cultivation, and promotion. It runs on a small, rotating committee and a fellowship of members progressing through a defined membership pathway.

The Problem

Not just "how well is the ministry doing," but: how much of what's working depends on the specific people currently here — and would it survive if they left?

A ministry can look completely healthy — great music, strong community, effective outreach — while quietly depending on one or two individuals for nearly everything. That fragility is invisible until the moment those people are no longer available.

What This Framework Does

  1. Diagnoses current maturity across three pillars — The Heart, The Art, The Mission — using a 4-level reproducibility scale.
  2. Surfaces gaps between where things stand today (As-Is) and where the committee wants to be (To-Be).
  3. Preserves disagreement as signal — individual ratings after group discussion, spread retained rather than averaged away.
  4. Feeds a 2–3 year action plan — out of scope for this design phase, but the destination.

背景

樂歌(LC)是一個跨教會的青年音樂事工,成立於2002年,圍繞四個策略目標而建立——傳福音、標準化、人才培育與推廣。事工由一個小型、輪替的委員會,以及一群依循既定會籍與服事資格制度成長的成員組成。

問題所在

不只是「事工做得好不好」,而是:現在運作得好的部分,有多少是靠現在這些特定的人在撐?如果這些人離開了,還撐得住嗎?

一個事工表面上可以看起來很健康——音樂好、群體關係緊密、傳福音有果效——但實際上可能悄悄地只靠一兩個人在支撐幾乎所有事情。這種脆弱在平常看不出來,直到那些人不在了,問題才會一下子變得非常明顯。

這個框架要做什麼

  1. 診斷事工目前的成熟度,涵蓋三大支柱——心、藝、宣——使用4級可複製性量表。
  2. 找出落差——現況(As-Is)與目標(To-Be)之間的差距。
  3. 保留分歧作為訊號——小組討論後各自獨立評分,分歧會被保留,不會被平均掉。
  4. 成為2-3年行動計畫的基礎——這一步不在目前的設計階段範圍內,但是最終要走到的地方。

What the Scale Actually Measures

The 4-level scale does not measure quality of execution right now. It measures dependency on current people vs. self-sustainability.

L1 Infancy
L2 Youth
L3 Maturity
L4 Reproduction

The threshold that matters most is L3 → L4, not L1 → L4. Something can be done excellently today and still score only L3, if it depends on one or two people with no active transmission mechanism.

Design Constraints Applied to Every Statement

  • Observable, not attitude-inferred. Statements point to a behavior a group can agree did or didn't happen — not an internal state like "joyful" or "cares deeply."
  • Single-barreled. Each statement tests exactly one thing, so a mixed reality has somewhere to go.
  • No scale/wording redundancy. Words like "consistently" or "regularly" are removed — the scale already expresses that gradient.
  • System, not outcome. Statements test a reproducible system LC controls — not an outcome like someone's personal conversion, or a quality trend like "is the music getting better." Reproducibility is the cause; improvement over time is the expected natural effect of getting the system right — not something worth measuring separately, and potentially misleading if you did (a quality uptick can come from causes unrelated to reproducibility, like a talented new member or an easier repertoire).
  • Ministry substance, not organizational mechanics. Governance, elections, onboarding compliance are out of scope.
  • LC's actual operational ownership. Statements stay within what LC itself can own — narrowed away from anything owned by a downstream party (e.g. a host church).

Architecture

3 pillars × 3 indicators × 2 statements = 18 statements total.

PillarIndicators
1 — The Heart (spiritual)Scriptural & Prayer Foundations · Ministry Identity · Community Care & Unity
2 — The Art (musical/generational)Vocal Development · Instrumental Musicianship · Conducting & Musical Direction
3 — The Mission (evangelistic)Outreach Intentionality · Total Mobilization · Event Structure Reproducibility

Pillar 2 was redesigned around the three role-types LC's music actually depends on (vocal, instrumental, conducting) rather than a generic function-based split, so each indicator maps to a distinct kind of person the ministry needs to reproduce.

Data Collection Method

Small-group discussion, but individual, per-person submission. Aggregation preserves the spread of ratings — "5 said Maturity, 3 said Youth" is retained as a finding, not smoothed into an average.

這個量表到底在測什麼

這4級量表不是測現在做得好不好,而是測這件事有多依賴現在的人,還是已經能自己延續下去。

1級 嬰兒期
2級 成長期
3級 成熟期
4級 傳承期

最關鍵的分界線是3級 → 4級,不是1級 → 4級。某件事現在做得很好,還是可能只拿到3級——如果它只靠一兩個人,而且沒有任何機制在教別人接手。

每句陳述都遵循的設計原則

  • 可觀察,不是用來推測內心感受。 陳述句指向一群人能夠對「有沒有發生」達成共識的行為——不是「喜樂」、「深深關心」這類內心狀態。
  • 一句只測一件事。 每句只測一個具體做法,混雜的現實才有地方被評出來。
  • 不跟量表本身重複用詞。 「持續地」、「經常地」這類字眼被拿掉——量表本身已經表達了這個穩定程度。
  • 測系統,不是測結果。 陳述句測LC能掌控的可複製系統——不是決志信主這類結果,也不是「音樂有沒有變好」這種品質趨勢。可複製性是「因」,水準隨時間進步是把系統做對後自然會發生的「果」——不需要另外去測,而且直接測反而可能誤導(水準提升也可能來自跟可複製性無關的原因,例如剛好來了一位有天賦的新成員,或曲目比較簡單)。
  • 事工核心內涵,不是行政運作。 治理、選舉、新人報到流程不在範圍內。
  • LC自己實際能掌控的部分。 碰到下游單位才能掌控的事,會被縮小成LC自己能掌控的部分。

Universal Rating Scale

LevelDefinition
L1 · InfancyThe fellowship cannot do this at all and requires constant outside support or leader drive to function.
L2 · YouthDoes this inconsistently; some parts function, many parts fail.
L3 · MaturityThe current core team has mastered this, but the knowledge is limited to them. They have not trained others.
L4 · ReproductionA clear system naturally encourages, trains, and produces the next generation to take over.

⚠️ Rating Discipline

Only score L4 if you can point to an actual, ongoing practice — mentoring, rotation, intentional modeling with explanation — transmitting this to newer members. If it's simply true right now but nobody is deliberately passing it on, the honest score is L3.

A high "Maturity" score should be read as a warning about hidden risk, not good news — it means the area is healthy today but has a single point of failure.

Worked Example — System-Type Statement

Statement 1.1: "The fellowship runs a consistent, structured Bible study series as a regular part of weekly gatherings."

LevelWhat a rater would observe
InfancyOnly happens on weeks the Director personally prepares and pushes it; without them, it doesn't happen.
YouthHappens most weeks, but patchy — some weeks solid, some skipped or rushed.
MaturityHappens consistently, well-prepared, good discussion — but only one specific person can lead it.
ReproductionMultiple people take turns preparing/leading it, and newer members are actively being coached into taking a turn.

Worked Example — Paired Statements (9.1 / 9.2)

9.1 Structured9.2 UnstructuredWhat it suggests
HighHighGenuinely reproducible on both fronts — the healthiest state.
HighLowWell-drilled on tradition, but no bench strength for creativity.
LowHighCreatively strong, but hasn't institutionalized its own recurring events.
LowLowBoth capabilities concentrated in one or two people — highest-risk state.

How to Score as a Committee

  1. Small-group discussion first — share perspectives and disagreements openly.
  2. Individual submission always — each person rates on their own, anonymously.
  3. Rate both As-Is and To-Be for every statement.
  4. The spread matters as much as the number — a wide spread is itself a finding worth surfacing.

通用評分量表

等級定義
1級 · 嬰兒期團契完全做不到這件事,需要外部持續協助或負責人強力推動才有辦法運作。
2級 · 成長期做得不穩定;有些部分還可以,但很多部分做不到。
3級 · 成熟期現在的核心團隊已經很會做這件事,但這個能力只有他們會,沒有教其他人。
4級 · 傳承期有一套明確的系統,會自然鼓勵、訓練並培養下一代來接手。

⚠️ 評分紀律

只有在你能具體指出一個真實、正在進行的做法——師徒帶領、輪替、刻意示範並解釋——正在把這個能力傳給新人時,才能打4級。如果現在真的存在,但沒有人刻意在傳承,誠實的分數是3級。

「成熟期」的高分應該被解讀成一個關於隱藏風險的警訊,而不只是好消息——表示這個領域現在看起來很健康,但其實只有一個(或很少)關鍵人物撐著。

陳述1.1:「團契每週聚會中,固定有一套結構性的查經(逐章研讀),作為固定的一部分。」

等級評分者會觀察到什麼
嬰兒期只有牧者/負責人親自準備並推動的那幾週才會有查經,沒有他就不會有。

傳承期好幾個人輪流準備、帶領,新人也正在被培養、練習接手。

委員會如何評分

  1. 先小組討論——公開分享看法與分歧。
  2. 一律個人獨立提交——每個人各自、匿名評分。
  3. 每一句都要評「現況」與「目標」兩個分數。
  4. 分佈跟數字一樣重要——分數分散本身就是值得討論的發現。
